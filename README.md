# EchoSelf 🌌

**AI-powered Emotional Time Capsules & Future Self Advisor**

> Something that doesn't really exist yet — and has real potential to go viral / become a top wellness + self-growth app.

## The Core Idea

Most "letter to your future self" apps are just dumb timers.

**EchoSelf** is different:

1. You create **Echos** — emotional snapshots of your current self:
   - Short text / voice note / photo
   - Mood + what you're feeling / fighting / celebrating
   - Optional context (goals, fears, wins)

2. The AI decides **when and why** an Echo should unlock:
   - When you enter a similar emotional state later
   - When you hit a related life milestone
   - When the system detects you need a "you've survived worse" reminder
   - Or pure resonant surprise

3. **Future Self Chat**  
   Talk to an evolving version of *you* trained on all your past Echos. It remembers your patterns, growth, and blind spots. It answers as the wiser version of yourself.

### Why this can actually become big
- Extremely personal → high emotional retention
- Shareable unlock moments ("My past self just hit me with this...")
- Daily utility + deep long-term value
- Privacy-first architecture possible
- Clear freemium path (more echos, deeper AI, voice, beautiful unlocks)

No mainstream app currently combines **context + emotion-aware unlocking** with an interactive Future Self trained on your own history.

## Prototype

This repo contains a fully working interactive prototype (vanilla HTML/JS + localStorage).

Just open `index.html` in a browser.

What works right now:
- Create Echos with mood + text
- Seal them
- Simulate AI resonance unlocks
- View unlocked Echos
- Chat with a simple Future Self that uses your Echos as context
